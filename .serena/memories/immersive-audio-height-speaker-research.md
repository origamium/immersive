# Immersive Audio Height Speaker Placement Research

## Overview
Research conducted via Perplexity on height speaker configurations for Dolby Atmos, DTS:X, and Auro-3D.

## Key Findings

### Speaker Types

#### Top Speakers (Ceiling-mounted)
- **Elevation**: 60-90° (nearly overhead)
- **Position Types**:
  - Top Front (TF): 60-80°, slightly forward of listener
  - Top Middle (TM): 70-90°, directly overhead
  - Top Rear (TR): 60-80°, behind listener
- **Best for**: Dolby Atmos native implementation

#### Height Speakers (Wall-mounted)
- **Elevation**: 25-50°
- **Position Types**:
  - Front Height (FH): 40-50°, on front wall
  - Side Height (SH): Similar elevation, at side positions
  - Rear Height (RH): 40-50°, on rear wall
- **Best for**: Auro-3D (25-35° recommended), DTS:X

### Format-Specific Recommendations

#### Dolby Atmos
- Prefers ceiling-mounted speakers at 60-90° elevation
- Forward offset of ~10° from directly overhead for front speakers
- Recommends flat ceilings between 7.5-14 feet

#### Auro-3D
- Height speakers at 25-35° elevation
- Creates "vertical stereo field"
- Optional "Voice of God" channel at 90° (directly overhead)

#### DTS:X
- Flexible, accepts both high (60-90°) and moderate (25-35°) elevation
- DTS Virtual:X can enhance non-ideal configurations

### Practical Considerations

1. **Ceiling Height**: 
   - Low ceilings (&lt;8ft): Wall-mounted height speakers preferred
   - High ceilings (&gt;14ft): May reduce effective elevation angles

2. **Multi-format Compatibility**:
   - Dolby vs Auro-3D have conflicting requirements
   - Modern receivers can remap for non-ideal positioning

3. **Ceiling Bounce Speakers**:
   - Require 8-9ft ceiling height minimum
   - Need consistent, reflective ceiling surface
   - Smaller sweet spot than direct radiating
